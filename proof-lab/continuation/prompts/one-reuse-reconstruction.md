Public mathematics. Attack this restricted synthesis theorem independently of scalar-profile reductions. A correct partial result is valuable; do not claim PvsNP solved. Give a complete algorithm and proof if possible, or isolate a falsifiable missing lemma.

MODEL: Unit-cost two-input NAND DAG, repeated fanins and unlimited fanout, original input signals/output selection free, no free constants or negations. Input is the complete truth table of a nonconstant f with d essential variables, and promise optimal NAND cost C(f)<=d. Target: uniformly reconstruct an optimal circuit using polynomial time in N=2^d, with no MCSP/SAT oracle. Reject or explicitly label broken promises. Exhaustive enumeration of all d-gate circuits is 2^{Theta(d logd)}, not polynomial in N, so it does not solve this task.

STRUCTURAL FOOTHOLD TO AUDIT: Discard unused primary variables by substitution with a retained essential variable (no gate cost), and prune irrelevant gates. For an optimal circuit with s gates and d essential inputs, the relevant multigraph is connected; repeated fanins count as distinct edges. It has2s edges and s+d vertices, giving edge excess2s-(s+d)+1=s-d+1. Every nonoutput signal has positive outdegree. Thus s>=d-1. At s=d-1 every nonoutput signal has outdegree1 (a read-once tree); at s=d exactly one nonoutput signal has outdegree2 and all others have outdegree1. This may be a reused input or a reused internal signal. Check all assumptions, including semantic cancellation and paid constants. The aim is to exploit one reused signal semantically rather than search arbitrary circuit descriptions.

POSSIBLE APPROACH TO AUDIT, NOT AN ASSUMPTION: A reused internal signal may be a read-once NAND function q(S) of a subset S of original variables. The rest is a read-once context except for two uses of q. Enumerating subsets or a fixed number of variable groups costs only 2^{O(d)}, hence polynomial in N. For any candidate S, the matrix of f over S and its complement must have at most two distinct rows if f factors through a one-bit q(S), so q can sometimes be recovered from the table up to polarity without enumerating2^{2^{|S|}} possibilities. The difficult next step is recognizing/synthesizing the two-use outer context with exact NAND cost, preserving polarity and reuse. Does the graph really imply the subset decomposition? Can a dynamic program over subsets/cofactors recognize the two-occurrence distinguished signal while all other variables occur once? Prove it or supply a concrete counterexample.

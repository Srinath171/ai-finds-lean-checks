Claim1: A nonzero rational polynomial P with t distinct nonzero monomials has t>=2^m if at a point p with every coordinate nonzero it has EXACT vanishing order m and its initial homogeneous Taylor part is C times the product of m linearly independent linear forms, C!=0.

Proof: Write P(z)=sum_alpha c_alpha z^alpha. In rational formal power series set z_j=p_j exp(T_j) and f(T)=P(p*exp(T))=sum_alpha v_alpha exp(alpha dot T), v_alpha=c_alpha p^alpha!=0. Since exp(T)-1 has linear part T, f's initial homogeneous part H is the original initial form composed with diag(p), so it is still C times a product L1...Lm of independent linear forms. Let V be the span of the t exponentials exp(alpha dot T); dimV<=t and every constant-coefficient directional derivative preserves V.

Choose directions D1,...,Dm dual to L1,...,Lm: Di Lj=delta_ij. For every subset S of{1,...,m}, D_S f lies in V and its lowest homogeneous part is C product_{j notin S} Lj, of degree m-|S|. This is because differentiating any higher homogeneous term |S| times leaves degree at least m+1-|S|. These 2^m series D_S f are linearly independent: in a nontrivial relation take the MAXIMAL |S| with nonzero coefficient. Its lowest degree is m-|S|; the terms of that degree are distinct complement monomials in independent L_j, hence linearly independent, contradiction. Thus2^m<=dimV<=t. Equivalently the argument uses the associated graded ('least space') of V, but no assumption that the initial-form map itself is linear is needed.

Finite-moment version: A_S,alpha=v_alpha product_{i in S}(Di(alpha dot T)), B_alpha,T=product_{i notin T}(Di(alpha dot T)). Then(AB)_S,T=D_S D_complement(T) f(0). Order subsets by increasing cardinality. If |S|<|T| the derivative order is<m, so entry0. If cardinalities equal, entry is C for S=T and0 otherwise (repeated/missing differentiation directions on H). Hence AB is lower triangular with nonzero diagonal C, is invertible, and forces 2^m<=t. This offers a finite alternative to infinite-series dimension language.

Claim2: F_m(x,y)=product_{i=1}^m x_i y_i(x_i+y_i) has MINIMUM GLOBAL MONOMIAL SPARSITY exactly2^m under every invertible homogeneous rational change A in GL(2m,Q).

Application: Let P(z)=F_m(Az), with intrinsic2D direct-sum blocks U_i spanned by the row forms l_i,r_i of A; three factor lines in block i are l_i,r_i,l_i+r_i. Need choose one line M_i in each block so their span R contains NO standard coordinate form z_j. Express the z_j basis in the l_i,r_i basis, yielding an invertible2m-by2m matrix. A nonzero determinant term assigns each z_j to one nonzero coefficient column, so at most two forms are assigned to each block. In block i avoid the at most two directions of their nonzero U_i projections, choosing among the three factor lines. A form assigned to i then has projection outside M_i, hence cannot belong to R=direct_sum M_i. Thus no z_j lies in R.

The common kernel K=R^perp has dimension m, and no coordinate vanishes identically on it. None of the remaining2m factors vanishes identically there either: each is a distinct direction in its own intrinsic block and so is outside R. Over infinite Q choose p in K avoiding this finite collection of proper hyperplanes. At p exactly m independent factors vanish and all other factors and coordinates are nonzero. Thus P has the exact normal crossing in Claim1, yielding t>=2^m. The identity basis attains2^m.

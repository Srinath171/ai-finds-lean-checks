Please first independently audit the following elementary partial argument (not Lean-formalized). Let V be the vector space of homogeneous linear forms. A linear derivation D is determined by its linear action V->V. If D(F_m)=lambda*F_m, reduce its Leibniz identity modulo any one of the 3m distinct nonproportional linear factors ell. Q[z]/(ell) is an integral domain, all other factors remain nonzero, so D(ell) is zero modulo ell. Both are homogeneous linear; hence D(ell) is proportional to ell. On each triple x_i,y_i,x_i+y_i this forces the same proportionality on x_i,y_i. Thus the full space S of such derivations is precisely the m-dimensional block-scalar space on the intrinsic 2D subspaces U_i=span(x_i,y_i). Conversely every block-scalar action scales F_m by three times the sum of its block scalars. Conjugation gives the same description after any invertible A.

Write P=F_m(Az)=sum_{alpha in E} c_alpha*z^alpha, all c_alpha nonzero, t=|E|. The diagonal Euler derivations D_a(z_j)=a_j*z_j belong to S iff a dot alpha is constant on E. Thus their subspace W has dimension 2m-rank{alpha-alpha0:alpha in E}, at least 2m-t+1. W contains global scaling and is a subspace of the conjugated block-scalar space.

If dim W>=2, for each intrinsic block U_i let chi_i:W->Q be its scalar action. Group blocks by equal chi_i. There are at least two groups (otherwise W consists only of global scaling, dimension1). Every coordinate form z_j is a common eigenvector for W. Its intrinsic-block components therefore lie only in one group; distinct chi_i cannot occur in the same such eigenvector. Consequently the coordinate basis partitions into bases of the group subspaces, and P factors on DISJOINT coordinate subsets into invertible changes of smaller F_k.
